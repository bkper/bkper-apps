# Bkper for Slack

Ask about your Books and work on them from Slack. Tag `@bkper` in a thread, or message it, and Bkper Agent (public
beta) answers right there, as you: with your own Bkper account, your access to Books and your name in Book history.

## Add it to your Slack workspace

1. Open [Add to Slack](https://channels.bkper.app/slack/bkper-slack/install). Anyone Slack lets install apps can add
   it; installing needs no Bkper account and grants nothing in Bkper.
2. Add `@bkper` to a channel and tag it, or message it directly.
3. The first time, it asks you, and only you, to connect your Bkper account: sign in with Slack, then with Bkper, and
   confirm. Everyone connects their own account the same way.

## What to know

- **Everyone acts as themselves.** Each person's own Book sharing decides what the bot can see and change for them,
  and their own Bkper AI allowance pays for it. Without access to a Book, it says so; it never borrows anyone
  else's.
- **In a channel, everyone in it sees the answers**, including people without access to that Book: you are sharing
  them. For anything private, message the bot directly. Direct messages are seen only by you.
- **Reply without tagging it** while it is talking with you in a thread. Once someone else posts, or you mention
  someone, tag `@bkper` again.
- **It asks before changing a Book**, and only the person who asked can confirm. To make sure it cannot change one,
  share that Book with them as View only.
- **Changes show as "you via Bkper for Slack"** in Book history, apart from Bkper Agent in the Book.
- **Files both ways.** Drop a receipt or a statement in a direct message, or in a thread and tag `@bkper`, and ask it
  to record it. Ask for a report or an export, and the file is posted in the thread below the answer.
- **Guests and people from other organizations** in shared channels use it the same way, with their own Bkper
  accounts.
- **Disconnect** any time from the bot's Home tab in Slack. Uninstalling the app removes everyone's connections.
