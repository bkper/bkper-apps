# File classification trial

Before extraction, Extractor Bot decides whether an uploaded PDF or image is a `statement`, which becomes one draft per row, or a `single_transaction`, which becomes one draft. It asked Gemini Flash-Lite. Could Gemini Decision 1, a System One decision model and the only one on the Bkper AI gateway that reads PDFs, make the same call as accurately, faster and for less? This trial asks both on the same synthetic documents. Following it, Extractor Bot now asks Gemini Decision and reads a statement from a probability of 0.8.

## In short

- Both models classified all 32 documents correctly, with no errors. That includes text PDFs, scanned PDFs, a 10-page statement and images. The set does not separate the two on accuracy.
- Gemini Decision read every PDF it was sent, as a file asset, with no rejections.
- Gemini Decision was faster: 891 ms median and 1,338 ms p95, against Flash-Lite's 1,404 ms and 5,263 ms. Four Flash-Lite calls took 4.5 to 5.3 s without retrying.
- Gemini Decision was about a third cheaper: $0.00031 against $0.00046 per document.
- Its probabilities separate the labels well. Every statement got at least 0.97. The closest single-transaction documents were the telecom "fatura" with call details (0.41), the hotel folio (0.22) and the timesheet invoice (0.18). Any threshold from 0.5 to 0.95 gives the same result here; 0.8 would leave room on both sides.
- Limits: 32 synthetic documents in one run, and Gemini Decision 1 is a preview model.

## Method

- 32 synthetic documents in `documents/`, rendered by `documents/render.py`, labelled in `cases.ts`: 13 statements and 19 single-transaction documents. Formats: 20 PDFs with a text layer, 6 scanned PDFs with page images only, and 6 PNG or JPEG images. 23 are in English, 9 in Portuguese; 4 PDFs have more than one page, up to 10.
- The single-transaction documents include those that look like statements: invoices with long or dated tables (a timesheet, a 2-page supplier invoice, a Brazilian NF-e and a telecom "fatura" with call details), a utility bill with a previous balance and payment received, a hotel folio, and bank-issued documents about one transaction (a wire confirmation, a boleto, a Pix receipt and a fee notice). The statements include those that look like bills: card statements with an amount due, a Portuguese "fatura do cartão", a statement with only two rows, and a payment platform's activity report.
- `gemini-flash-lite`: the bot's former classifier, kept unchanged in `flash-lite.ts` (system prompt, schema, model, minimal reasoning, retries), through `BkperAIResponsesClient` to `POST /v1/responses`. A document is a statement when the answer's `kind` is `statement`, as the bot routes.
- `gemini-decision`: one Open System One request per document to `POST /v1/systemone`, built by the bot's own `buildFileClassificationRequest`: the file as an asset (PDFs as `application/pdf` files, images as images), named in the state, and one `choice` question whose two criteria repeat the bot's system prompt. Flash-Lite's "when uncertain, choose single_transaction" is not in the question: code applies it as a threshold on the probability of `statement`.
- An error counts as wrong: the bot's parse fails and records no draft. Latency is wall time measured from the caller, including the network and any retries. Cost is usage times the gateway catalog price at recording time. Recordings replace each document's bytes with their SHA-256; scoring fails when a document or the bot's prompt has changed since recording.
